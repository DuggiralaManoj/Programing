What is method overloading and overriding?

Method Overloading:
           Defining methods with same name and having different number of parameters or different arguments within a same class.
 
--> But python doesn't support method overloading but we can archive that with default argument or *args in the parenthesis of the class.

Syntax for *args:

          #statements

Method Overriding:
