How to clear the screen ?

1.clear - to clear the screen.

2.tput clear or tput reset - to clear the screen in any Linux systems.

3.ctrl + L - from the key board also we can clear the screen.

4.clear - T <term> - to force the terminal type to be vt100. it is useful in scripting when the terminal behaviour is not stranded.

--> to know the terminal type "echo $TERM"

5.reset - not only clears the screen it also clears the terminal settings.

6.clear help - to get help and know the information about the command 

7.clear -V - to get the version  

8.clear -x - to just scroll down it not to clear.
